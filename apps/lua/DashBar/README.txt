Dash Bar - compact driver dash (CSP Lua app)

Install: drag DashBar.zip onto Content Manager and click Install
(or copy apps/lua/DashBar into <Assetto Corsa>/apps/lua/). Needs Custom Shaders Patch.

- Gear inside a ring that fills with RPM: green, amber once the shift lights start, red at the shift point.
- 15 shift lights (5 green, 5 red, 5 blue). All flash blue at the shift point.
- Speed, RPM and race position.
- Bottom strip: track temp, air temp and current lap time. After the line it shows your last lap in purple for 5 seconds.

Settings (cog on the app bar): KPH/MPH, where the lights start, shift point, max RPM override, how long to show the last lap.
Max RPM comes from the car's limiter; if a car doesn't report one, set it in settings.
